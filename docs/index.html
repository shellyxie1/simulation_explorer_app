Outlier Simulation Explorers

Two simulation studies of the same question: what happens to an exponentially tilted empirical likelihood (ETEL) fit when a share of the sample is contaminated? In each study the contamination level, the size of the shift and the replicate are chosen by the reader, and every view of that sample updates together. Both explorers are static pages over pre-exported simulation output, so a particular sample can be linked to directly.

Linear regression

A simple linear model $y = \beta_0 + \beta_1 x + \epsilon$ with $\beta = (2, -3)$. Outliers are observations whose error is drawn around a shifted mean $\mu_{\epsilon_{\text{out}}}$. Three estimators see the same data: ordinary least squares and two ETEL fits.

contamination1%, 5%, 10%
shift$\mu_{\epsilon_{\text{out}}}$ from $-5$ to $5$
replicates50 of 1000 observations
viewsone sample, sampling distributions, weights, likelihood surface

Log-normal distribution

A log-normal sample $y \sim \mathrm{LN}(\mu, \sigma^2)$ with $(\mu, \sigma^2) = (0, 0.25)$, fitted through its first three raw moments. Outliers have their log-mean shifted by $\delta$. Two ETEL fits see the same data, one started at the truth and one from a biased start near the edge of the feasible region.

contamination1%, 5%, 10%
shift$\delta$ from $-4$ to $4$, skipping $0$
replicates50 of 1000 observations
viewsone sample, sampling distributions, weights, likelihood surface
